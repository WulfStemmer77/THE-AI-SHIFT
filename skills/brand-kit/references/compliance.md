# AI-content transparency scope

Use current official guidance for the deployment jurisdiction. This reference reflects EU sources available on 2026-08-28 and is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## EU distinction

Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 applies from 2 August 2026, subject to the limited transition described by the Commission.

- **Providers** of relevant generative AI systems have machine-readable marking/detection obligations for generated or manipulated outputs.
- **Deployers** have disclosure obligations for specified uses, including deepfakes and certain public-interest text without human review/editorial responsibility.
- Not every ordinary generated image is automatically a “deepfake.” Scope depends on whether generated/manipulated content resembles existing persons, objects, places, entities, or events and would falsely appear authentic or truthful.
- A visible label or optional EU icon does not by itself prove full compliance.

## Operational decision record

Before applying a label, record:

1. jurisdiction and publication date
2. provider/deployer role
3. content type and whether it resembles a real person/object/place/entity/event
4. risk that it appears authentic or truthful
5. publication context and editorial review
6. selected visible and machine-readable measures
7. reviewer/approver

When scope is uncertain or commercial publication is material, require legal review rather than asserting compliance.
